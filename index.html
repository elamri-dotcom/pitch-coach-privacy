Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

The short version
  • Your voice is analyzed on your iPhone. Audio is never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Your recordings, practice history and settings stay on your device.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases go through Apple; RevenueCat helps us check your subscription status.

This policy explains how Pitch Coach (also listed on the App Store as "Vocal Pitch Monitor & Finder", "the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. If you have any question, contact us at the email address at the end of this page.

1. Microphone and audio

Pitch Coach uses your iPhone's microphone to show the pitch of your voice in real time. The audio is processed on your device to detect notes. We do not upload, store on our servers, or listen to your audio. You can turn microphone access off at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

2. Data stored on your device

The following stays in the app's storage on your iPhone and is not sent to us:

  • Recordings you make and songs you import from the Files app
  • Exercise scores, practice history, streaks and your vocal range results
  • Your settings (for example note names, tuning reference and appearance)

This data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which are controlled by Apple and you. Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Pitch Coach Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your name, payment card or Apple Account details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription or lifetime purchase. To do this, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier created by the app, your purchase and subscription history from Apple, and basic technical information such as your device model, iOS version, app version, country/region and IP address. This information is used only to provide and restore your purchases, not for advertising. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

4. Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from the app, your email app opens with a message addressed to us. It includes the app version, iOS version, device model, language and your current app settings, so we can fix problems. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. We use these emails only to reply to you and improve the app, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

5. Sharing

When you share a recording or your vocal range card, you choose where it goes using the iOS share sheet. If you save the range card to Photos, the app asks for permission to add images to your photo library; it cannot read your photos.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services that track you.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

7. Children

Pitch Coach is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for any personal information.

8. Your rights

Because your practice data stays on your device, you can view and delete it in the app or by deleting the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. To make a request, including about data RevenueCat holds for your purchases, email us and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
