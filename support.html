Purchases and subscriptions

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, open Pitch Coach, go to Settings › Pitch Coach Pro and tap Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore at the bottom of the Pro screen.

How do I cancel the weekly subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, choose Pitch Coach and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Will I be charged during the free trial?

No. The weekly plan starts with 3 days free. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged. If you don't cancel, the subscription renews at the weekly price shown in the app.

What is the Lifetime option?

A one-time purchase that unlocks Pro for good. It is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. Restore it on other devices with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using the app

The app doesn't hear my voice
  1. Open iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and make sure Pitch Coach is turned on.
  2. Check that no other app is using the microphone (for example a call or a recording app).
  3. In a noisy room, adjust Microphone sensitivity in the app's Settings.
  4. Sing or hum clearly, about 20–30 cm from the iPhone.
Can I sing along to music?

Yes. Turn on Keep music playing in Settings and use headphones, so the microphone hears your voice and not the music.

The notes look off by a little

Check the tuning reference in Settings (A4 = 440 Hz by default; you can set 435–445 Hz) and the note naming style (C D E, Do Re Mi, ドレミ or mid2G).

Where are my recordings? Are they backed up?

Recordings, scores and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes them, so share or save recordings you want to keep first.

Is my voice uploaded anywhere?

No. Your voice is analyzed on your iPhone and never uploaded. See our Privacy Policy.
